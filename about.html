Campus events, in one place.

UniEvents is a student-built platform for Obafemi Awolowo University — discover what's on, register in seconds, and walk in with a QR ticket.

For students

Browse by category, grab a free or paid ticket, and keep every registration under My Tickets.

For organizers

Apply to host. Once approved, create events, watch capacity, and scan tickets at the door.

For admins

Review organizer applications and grant dashboard access — no more informal WhatsApp lists.
